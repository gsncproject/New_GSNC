# The Four Horsemen
**Related Notes**:
- [[Hades]]
- [[Book of Revelation]]

The Four Horsemen are mentioned in Revelations 6:1-8, which is part of the New Testament's apocalyptic vision. When the first four out of seven seals are opened, these four riders emerge, each on a different colored horse. They all represent escalating forms of human suffering and divine judgement.
### Who are the Four Horsemen
###### White Horse (Conquest)
The White Horse is often interpreted as conquest or false peace. The horse's rider carries a bow and a crown, which symoblizes domination and/or the spread of power. (*Though the interpretation may vary in different traditions and cultures*)
###### Red Horse (War)
The rider of the Red Horse wields a great sword and is granted "to take peace from the Earth", this horsemen represents violence, conflict and bloodshed.
###### Black Horse (Famine)
The Black Horse's rider holds scales, which symbolizes rationing and scarcity. The horsemen as well represents economic collapse and hunger.
###### Pale Horse (Death)
The fourth and final rider is named Death, and Hades follows right behind. The horsemen represents mortality, plague, and overall destruction.
