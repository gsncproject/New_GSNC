# Strigoi
**Related Notes**:

Strigoi are undead or living vampire like being from Romanian Folklore, while the word itself originates from the Latin "*Strix*" and are known for shapeshifting, draining vitality and rising from the grave. Strigoi form the foundation for the modern vampire mythology.
### Types of Strigoi
As with so many different things there are two types of Strigoi's:
- ###### **Strigoi VII (Living Strigoi)**:
	- Humans marked from birth (e.g. born with a caul or as a seventh child).
	- Said to drain vitality unknowlingly or practice harmful sorcery.
	- (*Spirits might leave the body at night to feed*)
- ###### **Strigoi Morti (Dead Strigoi)**:
	- Back-To-The-Living corpses rising from the dead.
	- Known for blood-drinking, etc. (your typical vampire)
### Historical Accounts
One of the earliest recorded supposedly Strigoi cases is [[Jure Grando Alilović]] was considered the first historically documented vampire, who apparently terrorized his village after he died.
