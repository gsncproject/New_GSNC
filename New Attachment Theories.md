#gsnc #theories

The general idea of attachment is that there is a spirit that attached itself to a person and follows them for an amount of time. Often an attachment that be banished by conducting a kind of cleansing ritual but most of the time the attachment will stay with the person it is attached to.
## Regarding Sam Golbach's attachment
Ever since Sam and Colby have gone to the famous Sallie House, there's been the theory that Sam might has gotten an attachment from the little girl demon supposedly haunting this house. That is widely speculated due to him sitting on the burned pentagram in the basement, and doing this often occurring head tilt motif that was already seen in Jake Webber and Colby Brock. 

The Head-Tilt Theory started way back in the Biltmore Hotel in which it seemed as though Jake Webber got "possessed" during the séance they were conducting in the dining room.
