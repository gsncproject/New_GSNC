## The Picture Game
**How it works (simply)**: Gather some rope, scissors, small mirrors, an empty glass and a flash camera. Make a circle on the floor with the rope and place the glass in the center, filled with a bit of alcohol. Everyone surrounds the circle, holding mirrors with the reflective side upwards while closing their eyes in a dark room. While reciting the game’s [chant](https://www.scaryforkids.com/picture-game/), everyone passes around the camera, taking turns capturing pictures in the dark while declaring, “I caught you.”
