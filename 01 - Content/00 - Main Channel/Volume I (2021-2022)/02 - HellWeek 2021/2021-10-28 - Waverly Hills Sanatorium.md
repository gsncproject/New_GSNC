## The Hauntings of Waverly Hills Sanatorium 
- The Sanatorium was a hospital that treated one chronic illness in this case Tuberculosis
- Between 6,000-60,000 people died in Waverly 
- The Sanatorium was built in 1911 and operated until 1961
	- And up until the 1940s TB was incurable, so most people that came to Waverly died.
- If you have TB you basically drown in your own blood.
- The doctors started experimenting on patients to figure out how to cure the illness, e.g. they‘d replace your lungs with balloons.
	- All it cased were very painfull deaths
- Lots of people committed suicide because they knew that they would die anyway.
- There is a rumor about a seven year old named Timmy who fell off the roof and died because of that.
- Most of the spirits of Waverly are friendly and playful.
	- BUT there are exceptions: The Creeper 
- And Shadow Figures are common too, just like Doppelgänger
