The proposed index would ideally preserve:
- Exact response or phrase.
- Investigation and date.
- Timestamp.
- Communication method.
- Exact preceding question.
- Investigator who asked the question.
- Response type.
- Immediate interpretation.
- Other matching investigations.
- Contradictions and uncertainty.

The analysis should distinguish exact phrase repetition from broader semantic similarities and count distinct investigations as well as individual occurrences.
