## The central question: what happened to Sam’s attachment?
The investigation needs to distinguish between several possibilities:
- A. Sallie never left.
	- The entity identified as Sallie remained connected to Sam, even if later communication used different names or identities.
- B. Sallie left, but something else remained.
	- The original connection ended or changed, and another entity became associated with Sam.
- C. Sallie left and later returned.
	- The original connection ended or weakened, then potentially reappeared.
- D. Different entities have been identified as Sallie without sufficient evidence that they are the same.
	- The name may have been applied to multiple presences or communications that have not been conclusively connected.
These are working possibilities, not conclusions.
