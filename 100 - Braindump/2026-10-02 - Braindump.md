TikTok as a collaborative research space

Today’s discussion also highlighted something important about how GSNC works.

The TikTok comments are not just a place where I post theories. They can become a collaborative research space.

The September 20 exchange is a good example:

- I shared a theory and invited opinions.
- Ruth contributed information and a different interpretation.
- I brought in a connection she hadn’t known about.
- We recognised that the same name could point to different storylines.

This is valuable because other people may remember videos, names, sessions or details that I would never have found independently.

It also means different interpretations can coexist as separate leads until the evidence is examined.

Research principle: Preserve who introduced an idea, what they based it on, and how other people responded. Do not turn community speculation into established archive fact.
