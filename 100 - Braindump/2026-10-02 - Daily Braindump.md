### The existing Sallie/Lucy theories
The vault already contains several separate theories and questions:
- Sallie being attached to Sam.
- Lucy being identified as Sam’s attachment during the Stanley Hotel Return.
- Sallie and Lucy potentially being the same entity.
- Lucy potentially impersonating other identities.
- A possible demon or higher entity controlling multiple identities.
- The possibility that an attachment remained even if its apparent identity changed.
- The possibility that multiple entities were involved at different points.

These theories must remain distinct.

In particular, we need to avoid automatically treating the following as equivalent:
### One entity using multiple identities
An entity may communicate under different names or appear to imitate different identities.
### Multiple entities controlled by a higher entity
Several separate entities may supposedly be directed or influenced by another presence.
### One entity connected to multiple people
An entity may supposedly have connections to more than one person without necessarily being several identities or being controlled by something else.
### A changing attachment
A person may have experienced different reported attachments over time, or the interpretation of one continuing attachment may have changed.

These possibilities can overlap, but evidence for one does not automatically establish another.

**The central question**: what happened to Sam’s attachment?

We need to distinguish between several working possibilities.

#### A. Sallie never left
The entity identified as Sallie remained connected to Sam, even if later communication used different names or identities.
#### B. Sallie left, but something else remained

These are working possibilities, not conclusions. They are also not necessarily exhaustive. We should leave room for an explanation that does not fit any of these categories.

- Whether Sam himself describes a change, or whether the change exists only in how other people interpret the investigations.

We must also check whether any apparent changes actually began before Pendle Hill.
### An important chronological issue
The Stanley Hotel Return already contains the Sallie-to-Lucy identity problem in 2022.

Therefore, Pendle Hill cannot automatically be treated as the origin of every later inconsistency.

We need to establish what the attachment theory looked like immediately before October 2025 and compare it with what came afterwards.

A more rigorous way to investigate the attachment timeline
The next step should not be to add another theory.
It should be to reconstruct the timeline using consistent criteria.

For each relevant investigation, we should record:

| Category                | What needs to be recorded                                                        |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sam’s own statements    | What Sam explicitly says about an attachment, identity or experience             |
| Investigation questions | What Sam, Colby or another participant actually asks                             |
| Communication responses | Exact responses, including uncertainty and unclear wording                       |
| Witness observations    | What other participants independently report seeing, hearing or experiencing     |
| Interpretation          | What the people involved conclude from the events                                |
| GSNC interpretation     | How the event was understood in our own research at the time                     |
| Contradictions          | Anything that conflicts with the current interpretation                          |
| Continuity              | Whether there is a specific reason to connect the event to an earlier attachment |

This distinction is important because the following are not the same thing:
1. Sam says something is attached to him.
2. A spirit-box response gives the name Sallie.
3. Someone interprets that response as confirmation that Sallie is attached to Sam.

All three may be relevant, but they are different kinds of information.

We also need to distinguish changes in the reported experience from changes in interpretation.

For example:
- A change in what Sam reportedly experiences.
- A change in what investigation responses appear to communicate.
- A change in Sam and Colby’s interpretation.
- A change in our own GSNC interpretation.

These can happen independently.

A change in the theory does not necessarily mean a change in the reported attachment.

The period immediately before Pendle Hill

One particularly important task is reconstructing the period between the original Sallie/Lucy storyline and Pendle Hill.

We need more than the last investigation where someone explicitly says “Sallie”.

Questions:
- What was happening with Sam’s possible attachment during the intervening investigations?
- Were other identities introduced?
- Did Sam or Colby describe anything differently?
- Was the subject simply not discussed for a while?
- Were there reports of attachment attempts, communication or unusual behaviour?
- Did the interpretation change gradually, or was there a specific event associated with the change?

A lack of discussion is not evidence that an attachment disappeared.

Likewise, a later mention of Sallie is not automatically evidence that the original attachment continued.
