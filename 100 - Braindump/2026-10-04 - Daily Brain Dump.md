### Proposed convention
A useful general pattern is:
[Primary name] ([Context / distinguishing identifier]).md
#### Guidelines:
- Use a parenthetical when it helps distinguish an ambiguous or repeated name.
- Use a location when it provides a useful identifying context.
- A location means where the entity was first documented, not necessarily its alleged origin.
- A role or description can be used when a location is insufficient.
- Keep theories out of filenames.
- Keep aliases and additional names inside the note.
- Do not force parentheticals onto unique, unambiguous names.
#### Potential examples:
- Paul Miller (Villisca).md
- Paul (Farrar - boy).md
- Paul (Local School - reported janitor).md

These are examples of possible disambiguation, not confirmation that the three reported Paul references are connected.

## Part IV — RED JOHN: Working Codename for the Puppeteer Hypothesis
The Mentalist comparison

The user had been watching The Mentalist, particularly the Red John storyline around the beginning of Season 3.

The user noticed a structural similarity between the Red John mystery and the GSNC Puppeteer hypothesis.

The comparison was mainly about the Puppeteer, although the user considered both possible structures.

The two main possibilities

**A. RED JOHN is among them**
The hidden intelligence could be one of the apparently separate actors, hiding within the group.

In this structure, the identity of the controlling presence is concealed among the identities already known or suspected.

**B. RED JOHN is above them**
The hidden intelligence could be a separate presence that influences, directs, manipulates or operates through multiple apparently separate entities or agents.

The apparent actors might not even know who or what is behind the manipulation.

This is the structure most closely associated with the existing Puppeteer hypothesis.

Nested controllers

An additional possibility was discussed:

The apparent Puppeteer might itself be controlled by something else.

There could be intermediary layers, or multiple levels of influence.

The hierarchy might not even be simple: apparent actors could influence one another rather than all following one central controller.

In short: puppeteers all the way down is now an interesting possibility to keep in mind.

Working codename decision

The user explicitly requested that the Puppeteer be called RED JOHN for fun.

This is now the working codename for the GSNC hypothesis.

**RED JOHN**: A proposed higher-level puppeteer or coordinating intelligence that may influence, direct, manipulate or operate through multiple apparently separate entities, identities or agents.

The name is inspired by the fictional Mentalist storyline. It does not mean that the fictional Red John and a GSNC entity are the same thing.

The original term Puppeteer remains useful when referring to the underlying concept or earlier formulations of the theory.

Relationship to the four existing working models

The existing models remain separate:
1. One entity using multiple identities.
2. Multiple entities controlled by a higher entity.
3. Multiple identities emerging from a shared thoughtform or egregore.
4. One entity connected to multiple people or hosts.

RED JOHN primarily concerns model 2, but the hidden-among-them and nested-controller possibilities broaden the investigation.
### Research safeguards
Recurring names, voices or identities do not establish that one intelligence controls them.

Potential evidence of control would need to involve something more specific, such as:
- Coordination.
- Instructions.
- Influence.
- Shared objectives.
- Consistent relationships between supposedly separate actors.
- Evidence that one presence directs or manipulates another.

The fictional storyline is inspiration for a research question, not evidence for the GSNC theory.
