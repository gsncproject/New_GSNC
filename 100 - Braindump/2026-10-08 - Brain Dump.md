Part VI — Location-Origin vs. Investigator-Origin

A particularly useful distinction emerged from the Corvin material.

The investigation explicitly considers whether a spirit might be from somewhere other than the castle.

That question potentially intersects with:

- Attachment theories.
- Following theories.
- Poveglia.
- Objects such as the Kansas Warehouse box.
- Entities allegedly appearing repeatedly around investigators.
- The possibility of an entity being brought somewhere rather than originating there.

This suggests two different models:

Location-origin

Place → entity → investigators

The entity belongs to, or originates from, the location and interacts with investigators.

Investigator-origin

Investigator / object / previous location → entity → new location

The entity is already associated with the investigators or something they carry and subsequently appears elsewhere.

This distinction could become extremely important when comparing repeated entities across unrelated locations.
