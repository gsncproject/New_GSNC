# Adam‘s Theory (We Are The Ghosts)
The idea behind this hypothesis is that, what we consider a ghostly haunting, doesn‘t come from actual ghosts, but ourselves. Specifically from other versions of ourselves displaced across time, memory or reality. Ghosts aren‘t dead people who hadn‘t passed on yet, but its a living consciousness bleeding into moments they don‘t belong to yet.

Why of other versions of ourselves? In the [[Multiverses]] theory you have to think of a tree with lots of different branches, and each of these branches represent another time like, aka another version of one another.

Strong emotional events like death leave imprints - not just in places, but in time itself because these kind of emotions might echo across timelines and states, creating the illusions of ghostly experiences. That what we call ghosts could be simplified as a future or parallel version of someone unknowingly leaving traces behind in their past. So, it could be considered a haunting when someone unknowingly interacts with another point of their own existence — that could be moments in which you hear footsteps, seeing shadows, etc. - where different versions of the same consciousness quickly overlap.

I absolutely know where this is going and I hate it.

[[Retrocausality]] and some interpretations of [[Quantum Mechanics]] suggest that time isn‘t always linear and that information from the future can move backwards. If that is true then future events can already imprint themselves on the past, this means what we perceive as a ghost could be someone who hasn‘t died yet, someone who hasn‘t even been there yet.
