#### 2026-09-16
The Puppeteer Theory basically covers the question of, or more the idea that Sallie, Lucy, Emily, etc. could be connected to one bigger entity rather than being completely seperate entites.

So, instead of:
1. Sallie = one entity 
2. Lucy = another entity 
3. Emily = another another entity 
4. …

It could look more like this:
- One entity —> different names/identities/forms

And thats how the entire theory came to live. Because that could mean that all those names that we keep finding are just other puppets under the puppeteers influence, yet we have no idea who that puppeteer is.

One of the biggest question I have had since Lucy became part of this whole thing is:

**If all these names are connected, then who the fuck is actually behind them?**

Back in 2024 I had the idea that Lucy could be the one operating but yeah, I start to question that hypothesis.
