#### The Whole Theory Better Explained
Okay so hear me out.

I genuinely don’t think Sallie, Lucy and possibly Emily are three completely separate things. Like, everyone keeps treating them as separate because they’re called different names, but the more I looked at everything surrounding them, the more it started feeling like there was something connecting them.

The theory is basically that Sallie and Lucy are the same thing, just appearing under different names/identities. And Emily might be part of that too.

And before anyone goes “okay but why would it have different names??” THAT’S LITERALLY THE POINT.

If the Puppeteer Theory is right, then the names don’t necessarily mean we’re dealing with completely different entities. They could just be different identities being used by the same thing.

Which would make the whole thing way more complicated than just “Sam has Sallie attached to him.”

Because what if Sallie isn’t actually the name of the attachment? What if Sallie, Lucy and Emily are just the names we’ve been given for different versions of the same thing?

And THIS is where Sam’s attachment becomes important, because there are way too many moments across different investigations that make me go “okay why the fuck is this happening again?”

One of the biggest moments for me is the [[The Bellaire House]], when Sam literally asks whether Emily and Sallie are the same.

The flashlight responds in the way they were using it as communication, and then they basically just move on because something else happens. HELLO????? YOU JUST ASKED IF TWO ENTITIES ARE THE SAME AND GOT A FUCKING ANSWER.

Anyway.

Then there’s the [[2022-04-16 - The Stanley Hotel (Return)]], Lucy, the [[Estes Method]], and all the other weird overlaps that start piling up.

And this is also where things like “[[Alice]],” “[[Friend]](s),” and “It’s me” start getting interesting to me.

I’m not saying every time somebody says “friend” that suddenly Sallie is standing behind them or whatever.

I don’t know if this is actually what is happening. That’s the whole fucking point of the theory. I’m trying to figure out whether all of these things that look separate actually connect.

And honestly, the more I look into it, the worse it gets. I don’t think we’re getting out of this rabbit hole.
#### Theory Extension — Grace
This addition came from Grace (@samandcolbyfan833) on TikTok.

Grace's idea is basically that if Sallie, Lucy, and Emily are all different names/identities connected to the same entity, then we may have never actually found the entity's real name.

That could potentially explain why cleansing attempts haven't worked. If the name being used isn't the entity's actual identity, then trying to banish it by that name obviously wouldn't necessarily work.

She connected this to the Bell Witch investigation, where Sam was pulled out of the cleansing, interpreting that moment as the attachment preventing the cleansing.

She also pointed out that the attachment has supposedly been around for seven years, which she thinks could make it harder to get rid of.
#### Why This Is Interesting
This adds onto one of the questions I've been asking myself since Lucy entered the theory:

**Who the fuck is actually behind all these names?**

If Sallie, Lucy, and Emily aren't separate entities but different identities/forms of the same thing, then we've potentially been trying to identify the names instead of whatever is actually behind them.
