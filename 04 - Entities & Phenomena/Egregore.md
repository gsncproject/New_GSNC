# Egregore
**Related Notes**:
- [[Tulpa]]
- [[The Watchers]]
An Egregore is a non-physical entity/thoughtform manifested by the collective thoughts, emotions or intentions of a group. The word originates from the Greek word "*egrḗgoros*", and means as much as "*wakeful*"/"*watcher*" and is historically linked to [[The Watchers]] in the Book of Enoch.
### Two Important Historical Meanings
#### Angelic Watchers
In the Book of Enoch the *egrḗgoroi* are described as angelic being who decended to Earth to interact with the humans. Sometimes in older literature "an egregore" can also refer to fallen angeles.
#### Collective Manifestment
In modern Western Esotericism uses "egregore" to describe a psychic and/or spiritual construct, as well as a field of energy that shaües group identity
