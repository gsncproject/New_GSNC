>[!Quick Summary]
>Apparently there isn't just ONE Sam.

## The weird part
The different Sams aren't just random people who happen to look similar.

They seem to be distinguished by specific combinations of hair and eye colour:

| Sam | Hair | Eyes | Alignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sam 1 | Blonde | Blue | Evil |
| Sam 2 | Brown | Brown | Kind |
| Sam 3 | Brown | Blue | Evil |
| Sam 4 | Blonde | Brown | Evil |
| Sam 5 | ??? | ??? | ??? |

So basically we have almost every possible combination of blonde/brown hair + blue/brown eyes except Sam 5, whose appearance we don't know.

Which is... honestly fucking weird.

But I don't know yet if the appearance differences actually MEAN anything or if they're just how Malina's trance experiences represented the different Sams.
## Things I DON'T want to assume yet
- That these are actually different entities
- That Sam 1–5 are literally different versions of the same entity
- That their appearance determines whether they're good/evil
- That Sam 2 being scared of the basement means he's automatically good
- That Walter actually "summoned" them in a literal sense outside of what the storyline/communication says
- That Sam 4 being seen during the trance means the entity was physically present
For now, Sam 1–5 = separate identities/figures reported within the Malina material.

And Sam 4 is especially important because we have an actual trance event connected to a specific one of them.
