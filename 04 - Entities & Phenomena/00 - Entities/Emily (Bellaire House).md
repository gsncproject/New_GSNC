# Emily Davis 
## Who is Emily Davis?
Emily Davis is the name of a known spirit at the Bellaire House in Bellaire, Ohio. She is known to either be a spirit of a child who died tragically or a malevolent entity in disguise of a child. She is known to live on the second floor on the said house.
## Key Details
* **Location**: Emily inhabits the so-called “Emily Davis Room” on the second floor of the Bellaire House.
* **The Child Spirit Theory**: Some people believe that Emily was the daughter, of the founder of Bellaire, OH, [[Jacob Davis]], who allegedly died tragically. Most investigators use bouncy balls or light-up toys to interact with Emily.
* **The Demonic Disguise Theory**: A darker theory popular among paranormal teams suggests that there are no official records of an Emily Davis dying on the property. Instead, it is theorized to be a malevolent or demonic entity taking the form of a child to lower investigators' defenses.
* Phenomena: Associated with electronic voice phenomena (EVPs), sudden temperature drops, moving objects, and heavy spiritual manipulation within the home.
