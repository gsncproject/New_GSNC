# Tulpa
**Related Notes**:
- [[Egregore]]
- [[Tibetan Buddhism]]

A Tulpa originates from Tibetan Buddhism and refers to a mind-created form. 
### Traditional Tibetan Buddhist Context
The Tulpa (sometimes translated as *sprul-pa*) refers to a manifestation created through highly disciplined meditation.
### Western Esoteric Reinterpretation
In the early 20th-Century the Tulpa was first popularized as the idea of a thought-form that could take on autnomous qualities.
