# Biltmore Hotel Incident
**Related Notes**:

### Short Explanation
By now everyone who has started watching Sam and Colby has eventually reached the Millennium Biltmore Video where they stay at the Presidential Suite. And those who have watched it, argue whether it should be talked about more. But is it really worth talking about so thoroughly?

The most talked about moment isn’t merely those little signs at the beginning of the video while they were just exploring the room and telling us about the history of it. The most talked about part is towards the end of the video, as they were sitting at the table doing their little séance. Here’s a bit of background for as to why the Presidential Suite is so famous: The Presidential Suite sits on the tenth and eleventh floor of the hotel, while these two floors are said to be the most haunted, one of the most notable ghosts roaming these floors is Elizabeth Short also known as the Black Dahlia. Despite this haunted technicality the room is notorious for inhabiting famous people like Al Capone and Bugsy Siegel during the Prohibition Era. Other people like The Beatles and President John F. Kennedy stayed there as well. Needless to say, the hotel is prominent for many more reasons than simply being haunted by a couple of restless souls.

And then there is Sam and Colby’s Biltmore Hotel Incident. On February 24, 2019 Sam Golbach and Colby Brock, uploaded the video in which they, together with their friends Jake Webber and Corey Scherer, went back to the Millennium Biltmore for the second time. This time the plan was to stay the entire night, after they had to leave due to being too scared the first time. The four friends weren’t very experienced in the paranormal and such investigations and alike, it was more testing the limits after previously having an unexplainable encounter in Room B340 at the haunted Queen Mary Ship.

Throughout the beginnings of the video at the Biltmore, the group explores the hotel including going back to the staff door where its doorknob had rattled by itself the last time they had been there. And even in their room, the Presidential Suite, activity soon started to happen. Fans speculated that most of the objects making sounds in the suite were objects that Jake had previously touched earlier that night, such as the elevator door and the piano. What no one had expected at that time was that Jake might play a slightly bigger role than just “making” activity happen all around the Suite.

Now, towards the end of the Biltmore video the four friends sat in the dining room, trying to conduct a séance. They sat around the table with four candles placed in a sort of circle on the tabletop. Corey had been hearing things along, but no one else seemed to hear it while Sam had researched séances before they began with it. They all had their eyes closed and hands joined. They repeated “If there’s someone here, please use our energy to communicate with us.” Corey immediately after noticed how the chandelier started shaking, while Corey wanted to stop and leave Sam kept pushing that they have to continue and do the “summoning” once more, now that actual activity was happening. 

Lastly, they agree to do it a second time. This second time turned out to be a possible mistake, because after they repeated the same phrase, Jake slowly lifted his head while his eyes appeared to glow red, and after just a subtle gesture with his head all the candles blew out at once and the camera fell over. 
### Speculations regarding the séance
Only recently I began seeing Sam and Colby fans bringing this séance up once again, particularly after HellWeek 2024. Those who aren’t yet familiar with HellWeek; it’s a recurring event that happens annually on the Sam and Colby main channel, originally started in the year of 2021, is now a Sam and Colby Fandom Holiday. It began as a back-to-back saga leading up to Halloween with seven videos. Throughout the years there were lesser videos and longer waits between each. However, in 2024 Sam and Colby topped the bar at that time by buying the haunted Farrar Elementary School in Maxwell, Iowa. For HellWeek they stayed one week at their newly bought school to figure out why it is haunted. But the spirits inhabiting these school grounds and the adjoining cemetery weren’t very fond of them taking over the ownership of Farrar Elementary School.

The event most talked about was the Boiler Room Incident in which Colby was tied to a chair while Sam conducted a solo investigation in one of the school's classrooms. I won’t recount what happened throughout all seven days, but on day seven Sam proposed to Colby with his challenge for Colby. Around the same time they already knew that whatever was residing in the Boiler Room by the gym wasn’t merely a friendly spirit, so Sam gave Colby the chance to decide whether he wanted to take the risk or not. Colby took the risk. Now the most interesting detail about the time Colby spends in the Boiler Room isn’t the obviously stated such as him visibly being terrified in this situation. Here again comes in the fandom’s interpretations and speculations: Fans have pointed out a moment in which Colby looks slightly to the side before lifting his head slowly, almost the same way as Jake did in the Biltmore Hotel, which of course, got connected almost immediately. Furthermore, another connection, a third incident was connected to the Biltmore Hotel Incident and the Boiler Room Incident.

The Sallie House video was released on December 19, 2021. It was the last video Sam and Colby had uploaded in the year of 2021, it was sort of the year's finale. Together with their friends, Nate Hardy and Seth Borden, the duo went to Atchison, Kansas to investigate the supposedly haunted Sallie House. 

The Sallie House is said to be haunted by this six or seven year old girl named Sallie. The story goes: Sallie and her mother went to the doctor who was living in the house back in the day. Sallie was suffering from a very bad stomach ache, and the mother was seeking help for her at the doctor's place. After some examination it became clear that Sallie had appendicitis and that she had to have her appendix removed as soon as possible. The surgery took place in the very house, but the doctor hadn’t waited for the anesthetic to fully work before starting with the surgery. In the end Sallie died from the excruciating pain and with a resentment for men as the last thing she saw before she died was a man. But there is a big problem with this story and Sallie haunting the house. Speculations are that there are either two narratives or simply something darker disguising itself as a little girl. There are no records of a little girl named Sallie existing. That makes people believe that it's rather a darker entity pretending to be a little girl and with that luring people.

But why do I tell you this? Like the Biltmore and Farrar School video, towards the end Sam and Colby go into the basement of the Sallie House and Sam sits down on the burned pentagram. And there we have the head lifting a third time. 


The Sam and Colby fans have started to build a timeline with the help of those three incidents and have found that it was always three years apart: 2019 (Jake), 2021 (Sam), 2024 (Colby). Though, there is no solid evidence to call this something that is connected to each other, it has been a weird coincidence either way. Especially due to the fact that Sam Golbach does have a confirmed attachment identifying itself as “Sallie”, yet new speculations suggest that “Sallie” might not be the entity's true identity but a disguise.

  

Ultimately we can establish that we can not yet say with this little evidence if all these incidents are connected or not connected. But we can establish that it’s a coincidence which is weirdly consistent enough to be worth mentioning in this archive. We are yet to see what this truly means, and whether there will be another “head-tilt incident” in possibly 2027?
